Small resets

Short, doable things that tend to help more than willpower alone.

Take a screen break

Step away from your phone for ten minutes every couple of hours a short walk or stretch resets focus better than another scroll.

Protect your sleep window

Keep bedtime and wake time within the same hour each day. Consistency matters more than total hours.

Move a little, daily

Even fifteen minutes of walking or stretching measurably lowers stress and lifts mood.

Name what you're feeling

Putting a word to an emotion stressed, lonely, overwhelmed takes some of its intensity away.

Talk to someone

A five-minute check-in with a friend, family member, or counselor counts. You don't have to carry it alone.

Batch your notifications

Turning off non-essential alerts, even for a few hours, cuts down on the constant context-switching that drains focus.
